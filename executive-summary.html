Section · 04

Risks & Mitigations

Where to focus first

Top risk areas with mitigation pattern

Cleartext POP3 (high)Disable Post-by-Email or front the integration with a TLS-terminating proxy.
Cookie SameSite (medium)Migrate setcookie() calls to the array-options form to set SameSite=Lax.
MD5 derivations (medium)Track wp_hash and wp_salt usage; plan a hash-algorithm transition without breaking legacy nonces.
Login enumeration (medium)Return uniform errors at the login surface to mask username existence.
maybe_unserialize (medium)Treat option-table writes as trust-bearing; never persist attacker-controlled serialized payloads.
Defense-in-depth gaps (low)Most low findings are best fixed by output-time esc_html() and explicit LIBXML_NONET flags.
